Layout

Container, fixed header, element bars, stacking and side panel: the structure of a page. For columns see the grid.

Page structure

A page is a fixed .kit-header at the top and a .kit-main containing a .kit-container. Inside the container you use the grid or .kit-stack.

<header class="kit-header">
  <h1 class="kit-brand">Name <span>app</span></h1>
  <span class="kit-spacer"></span>
  <!-- buttons, menus, theme… -->
</header>
<main class="kit-main">
  <div class="kit-container kit-stack">
    …
  </div>
</main>
  • .kit-header: --kit-hh tall, stays stuck to the top (sticky). Table headers stick right below it.
  • .kit-brand: a title whose second word, in a <span>, is colored with the accent.
  • .kit-main: 24px of inner margin (16px below 900px) and 80px at the bottom.
  • .kit-container: centered content, at most --kit-container-w (1200px) wide. See Content width.

Content width

Narrow, centered content reads better for texts and forms; a dashboard with tables and charts often does better at full width. The kit allows both, at two levels.

ClassBehavior
kit-containerCentered, at most --kit-container-w wide. It follows the global switch (below): with data-layout="fluid" it fills the whole window.
kit-container-boxedAlways centered, whatever the switch.
kit-container-fluidAlways full width.

Global switch

Put data-layout="boxed" or "fluid" on <html> to choose the width of the whole page (boxed by default). With kit.js the user can change it from a button and the choice is remembered, like the theme. The width only changes when the window is wider than --kit-container-w.

<!-- full-width content by default -->
<html lang="en" data-layout="fluid">

<!-- a button that lets the user choose (it is remembered) -->
<button class="kit-btn kit-btn-ghost kit-btn-icon" data-kit-layout-toggle aria-label="Full-width content">…</button>
Kit.layout.set("fluid");   // or "boxed"; Kit.layout.toggle() switches

These documentation pages use kit-container-boxed and always stay centered. To see the switch in action open the sample dashboard: it starts at full width and has the button with the arrows at the top right.

Header aligned with the content

If you put a .kit-container inside .kit-header, it becomes the header's inner row and its content aligns with the page's, both centered and full width. Without a container the header always fills the whole window.

<header class="kit-header">
  <div class="kit-container">
    <h1 class="kit-brand">Name <span>app</span></h1>
    <span class="kit-spacer"></span>
    <button class="kit-btn kit-btn-ghost kit-btn-icon" data-kit-layout-toggle aria-label="Content width">…</button>
  </div>
</header>

Stacking: kit-stack

It lays out its children in a column with a constant gap (--kit-sp). It is the simplest way to put cards one under the other.

Element bar: kit-bar

Elements side by side and vertically centered, wrapping when there is no room: toolbars, filters, groups of buttons. .kit-spacer pushes what follows to the right.

kit-bar is not the grid's row: for columns use kit-row.

Side panel: kit-split

A list with, next to it, a detail panel that opens when a row is selected. Above 900px the panel sits next to the list and stays put while the list scrolls. Below 900px it covers the page from the right, with a dark veil behind it, and a click on the veil closes it. With kit-split-lg instead of kit-split the switch happens below 1250px, for lists that need a lot of room.

With kit.js three attributes are enough: data-kit-split-open="#id" on whatever opens it (a row, a button), data-kit-split-close and data-kit-split-full inside the panel. The second closes, the third toggles between "next to the list" and "full width" (the list disappears). With data-kit-split on the container Esc closes the panel, or brings it back next to the list if it was full width. On opening, focus goes to the panel and on closing it returns to whatever opened it.

The panel is made of kit-split-head (title in kit-split-title and buttons) and kit-split-body, which is the part that scrolls. Width next to the list: --kit-split-w (40%); width when it covers the page: --kit-split-overlay-w (860px). Without JavaScript just add or remove is-open (and is-full) on the container.

Moving to the previous or next row (arrows, "j" and "k") depends on the list and is not included: write it with two buttons and a handler that calls your own open function.

Base spacing

Three tokens govern the interface's breathing room (see Themes and tokens): --kit-sp (margins, distance between cards, inner padding, gutter), --kit-rad (corners) and --kit-ctl (height of buttons and fields). Below 760px --kit-sp goes down from 16 to 8px.
